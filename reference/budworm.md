# Budworm Data

Venables and Ripley (2002) state: "Collett (1991, p. 75) reports an
experiment on the toxicity to the tobacco budworm *Heliothis virescens*
of doses of the pyrethroid trans-cypermethrin to which the moths were
beginning to show resistance. Batches of 20 moths of each sex were
exposed for three days to the pyrethroid and the number in each batch
that were dead or knocked down was recorded."

Collett, D. (1991) *Modelling Binary Data.* London: Chapman & Hall.

## Usage

``` r
budworm
```

## Format

### 

A `data.frame` with 12 rows and 4 columns: –

- ldose:

  Dose of the pyrethroid trans-cypermethrin.

- sex:

  Sex of the budworm.

- numdead:

  Number that were dead or knocked down.

- numalive:

  Number that were alive.
